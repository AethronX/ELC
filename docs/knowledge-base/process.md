# Operating Process (Six Stages)

```
01 Supplier in China
        ↓
02 Pickup & Consolidation
        ↓
03 Inspection & Packaging
        ↓
04 International Shipping
        ↓
05 Customs Clearance
        ↓
06 Final Delivery in Oman
```

Etihad One should explain this naturally, in plain language, when a customer asks "how do you work" / "كيف تعملون" — not as a rigid script.
