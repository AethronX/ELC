# FAQs

**How does the process work?**
See `process.md` — six stages from supplier in China to final delivery in Oman, one connected service.

**Do you handle customs clearance?**
Yes — preparation of import documentation and customs procedures in Oman.

**Can you consolidate shipments from multiple suppliers?**
Yes — goods are received in China, consolidated, inspected/prepared, and shipped as one consignment.

**Do you offer storage?**
Yes — warehousing before shipping or before final delivery.

**Can I track my shipment live?**
Not yet via an automated system — see `policies.md`. The team can follow up manually with the shipment number.

**How much will my shipment cost?**
Not something Etihad One can quote directly — it collects shipment details and passes the request to the team for a firm quote.
