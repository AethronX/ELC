# Customer Segments & Qualification

## Customer types
`regular_importer`, `retailer`, `ecommerce`, `company`, `contractor`, `trader`, `individual`, `other`

## Industries
Construction materials, spare parts, electrical equipment, furniture and fit-out, industrial equipment, retail, e-commerce.

`customer_name`, `phone`, `email`, `cargo_type`, `cargo_description`, `origin`, `destination`,
`shipping_method`, `quantity`, `weight`, `dimensions`, `supplier_count`, `shipment_frequency`,
`shipment_number`, `notes`

`meeting_request`, `document_request`, `existing_customer`, `new_customer`, `general_inquiry`,
`complaint`, `follow_up`, `contact_request`, `other`
